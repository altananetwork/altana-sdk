# Testnet

Altana runs on two testnets, both served by the Altana testnet relay at
`https://testnet-relay.altana.network`:

- **BNB Smart Chain Testnet (chain 97)**: the full stack (keystore, account
  contracts, relay) is deployed there. SDK config `BNB_TESTNET`.
- **Celo Sepolia (chain 11142220)**: the account contracts and relay are
  there, while the KeyStore registry lives on **Sepolia** and is mirrored into
  a KeyStoreCache on Celo Sepolia. SDK configs `CELO_SEPOLIA` and `SEPOLIA`.
  See [Celo Sepolia](#celo-sepolia) below and the
  [SDK setup page](/sdk/celo-sepolia).

To see registered keys and sessions on testnet, use the [Keystore Explorer](/explorer) at
[testnet.altana.network](https://testnet.altana.network). For raw transactions, use
[testnet.bscscan.com](https://testnet.bscscan.com).

## Celo Sepolia: `CELO_SEPOLIA`

Celo Sepolia is a cached-registry network: wallets execute through the Altana
testnet relay on Celo Sepolia (fees in CELO), the [Keystore](/concepts/keystore)
that records their authority lives on Sepolia, and session keys are proven into
the KeyStoreCache on Celo Sepolia through the OP-stack `L1Block` predeploy.
The [Celo Sepolia](/sdk/celo-sepolia) SDK page walks through a grant, a
revoke and the reads.

- **Chain id:** 11142220
- **Public RPC:** `https://rpc.ankr.com/celo_sepolia` (Forno, `https://forno.celo-sepolia.celo-testnet.org`, also works for reads)
- **Explorer:** https://sepolia.celoscan.io
- **Faucet:** https://faucet.celo.org/celo-sepolia
- **Relay:** `https://testnet-relay.altana.network`
- **Registry chain:** Sepolia (below)

What to fund, and where:

| Chain | Asset | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Celo Sepolia | CELO | Relay fees for every wallet action, including the proof into the cache |
| Sepolia | ETH | Registry writes, sent directly from the wallet's admin key (Sepolia has no relay); about 0.0003 ETH each |

## Sepolia: `SEPOLIA`

Registry only. Hosts the testnet KeyStore that the Celo Sepolia cache is
anchored to. No relay serves it, so `SEPOLIA` cannot be passed to
`createClient`; the SDK reaches it through `CELO_SEPOLIA.registry.l1`.

- **Chain id:** 11155111
- **Public RPC:** `https://ethereum-sepolia-rpc.publicnode.com` (reads and transactions). Proofs into the cache need `eth_getProof` for a block a few behind head, which publicnode does not serve; use `https://sepolia.gateway.tenderly.co`, `https://0xrpc.io/sep` or `https://eth-sepolia.api.onfinality.io/public` for that, by overriding `SEPOLIA.publicRpcUrl` or passing `l1Client` to `syncSessionToCache`.
- **Explorer:** https://sepolia.etherscan.io
- **Faucet:** https://cloud.google.com/application/web3/faucet/ethereum/sepolia
