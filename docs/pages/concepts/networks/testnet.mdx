# Testnet

Altana runs on two testnets, both served by the Altana testnet relay at
`https://testnet-relay.altana.network`:

- **BNB Smart Chain Testnet (chain 97)**: the full stack (keystore, account
  contracts, relay) is deployed there. SDK config `BNB_TESTNET`.
- **Celo Sepolia (chain 11142220)**: the account contracts and relay are
  deployed there, the KeyStore is on Sepolia and a KeyStoreCache on Celo
  Sepolia mirrors it. SDK configs `CELO_SEPOLIA` and `SEPOLIA`. See
  [Celo Sepolia](#celo-sepolia) below and the [SDK setup page](/sdk/celo-sepolia).

To see registered keys and sessions on testnet, use the [Keystore Explorer](/explorer) at
[testnet.altana.network](https://testnet.altana.network). For raw transactions, use
[testnet.bscscan.com](https://testnet.bscscan.com).

## Celo Sepolia: `CELO_SEPOLIA`

Wallets execute through the Altana testnet relay on Celo Sepolia with fees in
CELO. Their sessions are recorded in the Sepolia [Keystore](/concepts/keystore)
and proven into the KeyStoreCache on Celo Sepolia, where they are read with
one `eth_call`. The [Celo Sepolia](/sdk/celo-sepolia) SDK page walks through
a grant, a revoke and a read.

- **Chain id:** 11142220
- **Public RPC:** `https://forno.celo-sepolia.celo-testnet.org` (Forno, the same endpoint the testnet relay uses)
- **Explorer:** https://sepolia.celoscan.io
- **Faucet:** https://faucet.celo.org/celo-sepolia
- **Relay:** `https://testnet-relay.altana.network`
- **Keystore:** Sepolia (below)
