# Welcome to Altana

Altana is noncustodial infrastructure that lets your AI agents pay, invest, and operate on your behalf — always inside limits you set and can revoke at any moment. Every permission lives in a neutral onchain registry: openly verifiable, revocable in one transaction, and accessible by any agent.
