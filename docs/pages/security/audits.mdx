# Audit reports

The Altana Keystore contracts were audited by [CertiK](https://www.certik.com), completed 15 July 2026. The full report, the finding-by-finding detail and the current status of every item are published on [CertiK Skynet](https://skynet.certik.com/projects/altana).

Scope was `KeyStore.sol` and `KeyStoreCacheOPStack.sol`, plus six further files listed in the report: the onchain registry that stores session-key permissions, and the L2 cache contract that mirrors them to an L2. These are the contracts that decide whether an agent is authorized to act.

The KeyStore is deployed from the audited source and source-verified, exact match, on Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain. The cache on Celo is KeyStoreCacheOPStack 1.1.1: the audited contract with one fix made after the audit, on 19 August 2026, to how it decodes the storage value a proof carries. Its source is verified, exact match, on Sourcify. You can read each one directly:
