# Audit reports

The Altana Keystore contracts were audited by [CertiK](https://www.certik.com), completed 15 July 2026. The full report, the finding-by-finding detail and the current status of every item are published on [CertiK Skynet](https://skynet.certik.com/projects/altana).

Scope was `KeyStore.sol` and `KeyStoreCacheOPStack.sol`, plus six further files listed in the report: the onchain registry that stores session-key permissions, and the L2 cache contract that mirrors them to Base. These are the contracts that decide whether an agent is authorized to act.

The audited source is deployed and source-verified, exact match, on all three networks Altana runs on. You can read it directly:
