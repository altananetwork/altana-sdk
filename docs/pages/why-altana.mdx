# Why Altana

Altana enables a **global registry of permissions onchain, accessible by any agent**.

Traditional agentic wallets store permissions locally or in centralized servers.<br />Altana's **Keystore** infrastructure makes composable permissions accessible across any chain and any wallet, enabling:
